What is an EERD?

An Enhanced ERD (EERD) extends the standard ERD with additional concepts to model more complex real-world relationships:

  • Specialization — Breaking a general entity into more specific subtypes (top-down)
  • Generalization — Combining similar entities into a supertype (bottom-up)
  • Total vs. Partial participation — Whether every instance of the supertype must belong to a subtype
  • Disjoint vs. Overlapping — Whether a supertype instance can belong to multiple subtypes at once
Total Specialization

Every USERS instance must belong to at least one subtype. Denoted with a double line to the circle.

Used for: Admin, Secretary, Staff
Partial Specialization

A USERS instance may or may not be linked to a Resident record. Denoted with a single line.

Used for: Resident Account
Disjoint Constraint

A user can only have one role at a time — admin, secretary, staff, or resident. Denoted with "d" in the circle.

Exclusive roles

Specialization Summary — USERS Supertype
Subtype Participation Constraint Additional Attributes / Behaviors
ADMIN Total Disjoint Can manage system settings, users, and view all activity logs.
SECRETARY Total Disjoint Can issue and manage certificate requests on behalf of residents.
STAFF Total Disjoint Read-only access; can view residents and certificates but cannot issue them.
RESIDENT_ACCOUNT Partial Disjoint Linked to a RESIDENTS record. Can submit own certificate requests and track status.
